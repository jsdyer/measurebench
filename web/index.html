Choose the right unit

GB uses 1,000³ bytes; GiB uses 1,024³. A “1 TB” drive is about 931 GiB before formatting. This is a unit difference, not missing capacity.

Cups and gallons need US, metric or imperial explicitly. Supported units include acres, hectares, nautical miles, knots, smoots, hands and food calories.
